Key findings

  • Coin sinks recover about 63% of the coins minted. Prices were not measured.
  • Two premium items, Phoenix Blade (61% win rate) and Aegis Shield (59%), are overpowered by a meaningful margin (CIs above 50% and ≥5 points off it). Items sitting 1–2 points above 50% test "significant" only because of large sample sizes, so they are flagged "skewed, small" rather than overpowered — effect size, not just the p-value.
  • Retention falls sharply after the first day: 47% return on day 1, 18% by day 7, and 4% by day 30.
  • A classifier using days 0–3 activity reaches ROC AUC 0.730 and average precision 0.092 for first purchases during days 4–30, versus a 0.028 prevalence baseline. Features use only days 0–3; the 5,982 eligible players have not paid before day 4. The synthetic generator partly determines the relationship; an offer still needs an experiment.
Recommendation: Test coin sinks to reduce the supply surplus, rebalance the two overpowered premium items, and invest in the first-day experience to slow the early drop in retention. (Synthetic data with designed-in ground truth — the value is the method.)
